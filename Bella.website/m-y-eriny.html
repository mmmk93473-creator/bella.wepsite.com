About M/Y Eriny

At 33 metres, M/Y Eriny is a modern Red Sea liveaboard offering comfort and effortless diving. She accommodates 20 guests in 10 air-conditioned cabins with private bathrooms.

A spacious salon, shaded sun deck, and well-equipped dive deck with air and Nitrox ensure smooth days at sea. Built to international safety standards and supported by an experienced crew, Eriny delivers safe, enjoyable diving across the Red Sea’s famous reefs and wrecks.

The M/Y Eriny Experience

M/Y Eriny offers spacious, air-conditioned interiors with comfortable social areas, audiovisual entertainment, panoramic sun decks, and full relaxation spaces between dives.

Spacious Air-Conditioned Salon

Relax in our climate-controlled salon featuring plush seating, widescreen TV, media library with movies, and marine life reference books for dive logging.

Free Wi-Fi in Public Areas

Stay connected with complimentary Wi-Fi available in the salon and public deck areas (subject to local Red Sea coastal network coverage).

Shaded Sun Deck & Loungers

Multiple teak sun decks offering both shaded seating areas and open sun loungers for soaking up the Egyptian sunshine and stargazing at night.

Bar & Social Lounge

Enjoy refreshing beverages, fruit juices, welcome cocktails, and evening social hours with your fellow dive travelers and guides.

Freshwater Deck Showers

Warm freshwater rinse showers on the dive platform allow immediate rinsing after surfacing from your dives.

⚡

Twin Zodiac Tenders

Two powerful zodiac chase boats equipped with 40HP engines provide swift, comfortable drop-offs and pick-ups at pristine reef walls and pinnacles.

DIVING INFRASTRUCTURE

Diving with M/Y Eriny

Designed by divers for divers. M/Y Eriny’s expansive dive deck and low swim platform provide stress-free gearing up, smooth tender entries, and dedicated camera stations.

  • 🤿 Nitrox Available: Continuous blending system onboard
  • 🔋 15L Cylinders: Available for deep/heavy-air divers upon request
  • 🚤 Twin Diving Tenders: 2 fast RIB tenders with 40HP engines
  • 🔧 DIN / INT Adaptors: Compatible with all international regulator setups
  • 🌊 Sidemount Diving: Supported with ample tank rigging stations
  • 📷 Camera Care: Dedicated rinse tanks and air blow guns
