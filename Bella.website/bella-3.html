About Bella 3

At 32 metres, Bella 3 offers a comfortable, dive-focused setup with air-conditioned cabins, a spacious salon and dining area, free Wi-Fi, and a shaded sun deck.

The large dive deck includes organised gear storage, a dedicated camera table, rinsing tanks, and both air and continuous-blended Nitrox. Designed for stability and guest convenience across three generous decks.

Perfect for shorter Mini Safaris, she explores classic Red Sea sites including the Straits of Tiran, Ras Mohammed National Park, and the world-renowned SS Thistlegorm and SS Dunraven shipwrecks.

The Bella 3 Experience

Explore the carefully designed social and diving zones aboard Bella 3, created for maximum relaxation between world-class Red Sea dives.

Shaded Sun Deck

Generous open-air sun deck fitted with cushioned loungers, shaded pergola seating, and panoramic 360-degree views of the Red Sea horizons.

Upper Deck Sitting Area

Inviting outdoor mid-deck lounge area for dive debriefs, evening drinks, and admiring the Sinai coastal sunset breezes.

The Salon & Lounge

Air-conditioned indoor saloon featuring comfortable plush sofas, media entertainment centre, marine reference books, and beverage bar.

Dining Restaurant

Dedicated dining hall serving fresh buffet-style international cuisine, Egyptian specialties, and made-to-order snacks between dives.

Custom-Built Dive Deck

Ergonomic dive deck with individual kitting stations, camera setup table, dual freshwater rinse tanks, and seamless wide swim platform entry.

Leisure Deck & Outdoor Bar

Open-air relaxation deck equipped with an outdoor bar serving chilled soft drinks, fresh fruit juices, and post-dive refreshments.
