About Bella 2

At 31 metres, Bella 2 is the most intimate liveaboard in our fleet, offering a relaxed and friendly atmosphere tailored for shorter Mini Safaris and northern Red Sea exploration.

With cozy indoor spaces, welcoming social decks, and an efficient onboard layout, Bella 2 provides a personalized, boutique liveaboard experience. Designed for smooth, comfortable diving in the northern Red Sea, she is ideal for divers who appreciate smaller groups and flexible safari schedules.

LIFE ONBOARD

The Bella 2 Experience

Every space aboard Bella 2 is thoughtfully arranged for relaxation between dives, with shaded decks, air-conditioned salons, and panoramic sea views.

The Salon & Lounge

Comfortable, air-conditioned indoor salon featuring plush seating, multimedia entertainment, marine library, and beverage service.

Shaded Sun Deck & Loungers

Generous teak sundeck with cushioned sunbeds, shaded lounging zones, and 360-degree panoramic views of Sinai coastal sunsets.

Upper Deck Sitting Area

Open-air mid-deck social area ideal for logbook debriefs, fresh ocean breezes, and evening star gazing with fellow divers.

Dining Restaurant

Dedicated dining hall serving fresh buffet-style meals, international cuisine, and local Egyptian delicacies prepared three times daily.

Custom-Built Dive Deck

Spacious dive deck equipped with individual gear stations, freshwater rinse tanks, twin zodiac tenders, and seamless water access.

Top Observation Deck

Elevated vantage point offering tranquil relaxation, sea-breeze sunbathing, and panoramic vistas of the Straits of Tiran.

DIVING CAPABILITIES

Diving with Bella 2

Bella 2 provides smooth, comfortable diving access to northern Red Sea jewels including Ras Mohammed National Park, the Straits of Tiran, the SS Thistlegorm, and the Dunraven.

  • 🤿 Nitrox Available: Enriched Air fills available onboard
  • 🚤 2 Inflatable Tenders: Fast RIBs for reef drop-offs and drift pickups
  • ☀️ Shaded Diving Area: Ample sun protection during surface prep
  • 🔧 DIN Adaptors: Compatible with all international regulator systems
  • 🌊 Mini Safari Specialists: Flexible 3 to 5-day safari routes
