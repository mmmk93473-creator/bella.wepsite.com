WWII SHIPWRECK

SS Thistlegorm

Sunk in 1941 by German Heinkel bombers, the 128m British merchant vessel sits upright at 30m depth. Divers can penetrate holds packed with Bedford trucks, BSA motorbikes, munitions, locomotives, and encounter schooling batfish and giant morays.

Bedford Trucks BSA Motorbikes Night Dives Nitrox Recommended
Depth Range16 - 32 Metres
Required LevelAdvanced Open Water (20+ Dives)
Key Marine LifeBatfish, Barracudas, Giant Trevally

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Helpful verified answers about airport transfers, diving qualifications, onboard dining, and liveaboard life.

Yes, we arrange private airport transfers between Sharm El Sheikh International Airport (SSH) and Travco Marina for €30 each way. Simply provide your flight number at least 48 hours prior to arrival, and our trusted driver will greet you at arrivals.
For Northern routes (Ras Mohammed, Tiran, Thistlegorm), Open Water Diver with at least 15-20 logged dives is recommended (Advanced Open Water required for deep wreck penetration at 30m). For Brothers Islands routes, Egyptian maritime regulations require a minimum of 30 logged dives.
Yes, all Bella Liveaboard vessels (M/Y Eriny, Bella 1, Bella 2, Bella 3) are equipped with continuous blending Nitrox systems and analyzer stations. Certified Enriched Air divers can dive Nitrox, and PADI Nitrox courses can also be completed onboard.
All trips are Full Board: 3 freshly prepared buffet meals daily (breakfast, lunch, dinner), post-dive afternoon snacks, fresh fruits, mineral water, tea, and instant coffee. Soft sodas are available, and special dietary requests (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) are catered to upon advance notice.
All standard trips depart from and return to Travco Marina in Sharm El Sheikh, South Sinai, Egypt. Boarding typically starts at 18:00 on the departure day, with yacht departure early the following morning.
