About Bella 1

At 35 metres, Bella 1 is a comfortable Red Sea liveaboard hosting 22 guests in 11 air-conditioned cabins with private bathrooms.

She offers relaxed lounges, a shaded sun deck, and a well-organized dive deck with air and Nitrox. With an experienced crew and easy onboard layout, Bella 1 is an ideal base for exploring Tiran, Ras Mohammed, the SS Thistlegorm, Dunraven, Abu Nuhas, and the Brothers.

ONBOARD COMFORTS

The Bella 1 Experience

Bella 1 features air-conditioned interiors with a comfortable salon, TV, and dive guide library, plus free Wi-Fi and all-day refreshments. The sun deck offers cushioned loungers, and the spacious dive deck includes organized gear storage, twin zodiacs, a camera table, rinse tanks, and air and Nitrox for smooth Red Sea diving.

DIVING INFRASTRUCTURE

Diving with Bella 1

Bella 1 was custom-built specifically for Red Sea diving safaris. Her large dive platform, individual tank stations, and dual chase ribs provide effortless diving across Egypt’s most challenging drift reefs and deep shipwrecks.

  • 🤿 Nitrox Available: Enriched Air continuous blending system
  • 🛥️ Twin Zodiacs: 2 dedicated tenders for fast entry & pick-ups
  • 🗄️ Organized Gear Storage: Dedicated numbered bins for each diver
  • 📷 Camera Table: Dedicated charging and rinse facilities
  • 🚿 Deck Showers: Warm freshwater rinse showers
  • 📚 Dive Guide Library: Briefing maps and species identification
